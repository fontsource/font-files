Uncut Sans is a slightly quirky sans-serif by Kasper Nordkvist, originally designed for UNCUT.wtf. Rather than reproducing a particular historical model, it embraces unexpected details and a deliberately hard-to-place personality that can bring character to display, editorial, and identity work.

The variable family covers a broad range of weights and upright-to-italic styles. OpenType stylistic sets provide alternate letters, figures, punctuation marks, rounded punctuation, and arrows.
