Monostain is a free monospace font from the Stain family, inspired by JetBrains Mono. Every glyph is generated in code: centerlines are stroked at each weight's width and merged into outlines, with arches and bowls drawn as filled bands so they thin into their stems. It comes in Regular, Bold, Italic and Bold Italic, with 383 characters covering ASCII, Latin-1, Latin Extended-A, punctuation, math, arrows and box drawing.
