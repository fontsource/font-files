Goeist Mono is the monospaced partner to Goeist, by Aniket Pawar, developed under
Shadcn Labs and based on Geist Mono by Vercel. Every exposed corner is drawn as
if by one round tool: stroke endings, slabs and dots become semicircles and
apexes are fully rounded, while widths stay fixed. It keeps Geist Mono's coding
ligatures, for code editors, terminals and other text-based interfaces.
